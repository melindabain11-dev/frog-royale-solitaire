# 🐸 Frog Royale Solitaire 💞

A whimsical, romantic solitaire puzzle featuring your custom Grok Imagine frog playing cards!

**The Story:**  
In the enchanted misty swamps, the regal **King of Spades** and elegant **Queen of Hearts** have found their perfect match in the charming **Frog Lovers** from the tropical beach. Help arrange them on the lily pads so their love can truly bloom through the magic of patience and perfect stacking.

## How to Play

- There are 5 beautiful lily pad positions.
- The three royal cards start randomly placed on three of them.
- Drag the top card from any pad to another pad.
- You can place a card on an empty lily pad anytime.
- You can stack a card onto another only if it follows the "royal courtship" rules:
  - The card must be **lower rank** than the one it's placed on.
  - Preferably **opposite color** (black spade on red heart, etc.)
  - Special romantic exception: The **Lovers** card can always be placed on the **Queen of Hearts** (love conquers all!)
- **Win** by uniting all three in one beautiful stack: King at the bottom, Queen in the middle, Lovers on top.

The King and Queen can be stacked first (valid move), then bring the Lovers to complete their royal union.

## Adding More Cards (Future Expansion)

This is version 1 with your three gorgeous custom cards. To expand into a full 52-card Klondike solitaire:
1. Generate more frog-themed cards in the exact same artistic style using Grok Imagine.
2. Add them to the `images/` folder with consistent naming (e.g. `ace-hearts.png`, `2-spades.png`).
3. Extend the `cards` array and ranks in `index.html`.
4. We can upgrade the mechanics together for full tableau building, stock/waste, foundations, and scoring!
